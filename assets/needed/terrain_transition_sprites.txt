Terrain transition artwork still needed for authored, non-geometric borders:

- Supplied globally reusable masks now cover horizontal edges, vertical edges, and concave corners.
- Supplied corner masks now include concave, convex-large, A-dominant, B-dominant, and even variants.
- No combined or multi-edge mask is currently required. Opposing edges and junctions
  are composed from multiple existing masks in terrain-priority order. Add a new
  authored mask only after recording an exact neighbor configuration that cannot be
  represented by this composition.
- Optional later layers: wet sand, shoreline foam animation, moss, leaves, and rock foam.

The runtime currently uses the higher-priority terrain's base texture on small overlay
planes. Recipes already support multiple layers and can point at these sprites later.
